<div style="text-align: center; font-style: italic;">
Who decides, who lives, who dies? <br>
Who leaves behind the teary-eyed, <br>
Where we just watch, as passerby, <br>
Her journey to the Other Side. <br>

Who decides, who lives to cry? <br>
To comb through photos, to stand in line. <br>
To be greeted by family and friends, <br>
Who know what it’s like to learn to live again.  <br>

What does it mean, to live, then die? <br>
To go, not gently, into that good night. <br>
When we last spoke, I held back tears, <br>
And she told me, “I’ll always be here”. <br>

What does it mean, to live so bright? <br>
To touch so many, to change our lives. <br>
Perhaps life is not measured up to its close, <br>
But by the stories yet to be told.  <br>

So the challenge now is to live for her. <br>
To give her more time on this precious Earth. <br>
For one is only, truly gone, <br>
When no one is left to sing their song.  <br>

So we carry her with us in the time to come.  <br>
We show her joy, we show her love.  <br>
She is with us now, she sits in these seats, <br>
For we are her new heartbeat.  <br>
</div>


Written in loving memory of my grandmother, Menghi.
